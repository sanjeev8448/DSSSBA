
## 1. Simple Average

The average (arithmetic mean) is the total of all observations divided by the number of observations.

**Formula:**

Average = Sum of observations / Number of observations

A useful rearrangement:

Total = Average × Number of observations

### Example 1: Calculate Average

Find the average of 12, 18, 20, 25 and 30.

Sum = 12 + 18 + 20 + 25 + 30 = 105

Average = 105 / 5 = 21

**Answer: 21**

### Example 2: Find the Sum

The average of 8 numbers is 15. Find their sum.

Sum = 8 × 15 = 120

**Answer: 120**

### Average of Consecutive Numbers

For consecutive numbers or equally spaced numbers in an arithmetic sequence:

Average = (First number + Last number) / 2

Example: Find the average of 11, 12, 13, 14 and 15.

Average = (11 + 15) / 2 = 13

**Answer: 13**

This shortcut also works for equally spaced numbers such as 5, 10, 15, 20 and 25.

### Effect of Adding or Removing a Number

If the average of n numbers is A:

Original total = n × A

- When a new number x is added:
    
    New average = (n × A + x) / (n + 1)
    
- When a number x is removed:
    
    New average = (n × A − x) / (n − 1)
    

The removal formula applies when n > 1.

**Example:** The average of 5 numbers is 20. A sixth number, 32, is added. Find the new average.

Original sum = 5 × 20 = 100

New sum = 100 + 32 = 132

New average = 132 / 6 = 22

**Answer: 22**

**Shortcut:** When a number is added, the average moves towards that number. If the added number is greater than the old average, the average increases.

## 2. Weighted Average

A weighted average is used when different values have different weights, frequencies, quantities, or importance.

**Formula:**

Weighted average = Sum of (Value × Weight) / Sum of weights

### Example 1: Average Marks

A student scores 70 marks in a test worth 40% of the final grade and 80 marks in a test worth 60%. Find the weighted average.

Weighted average = [70 × 40 + 80 × 60] / (40 + 60)

= (2,800 + 4,800) / 100

= 7,600 / 100

= 76

**Answer: 76 marks**

### Example 2: Different Quantities

A shopkeeper buys 2 kg of rice at ₹40/kg and 3 kg at ₹50/kg. Find the average price per kg.

|Purchase|Quantity|Price per kg|Total cost|
|---|---|---|---|
|First|2 kg|₹40|₹80|
|Second|3 kg|₹50|₹150|
|Total|5 kg|—|₹230|

Average price = Total cost / Total quantity

= 230 / 5 = ₹46/kg

**Answer: ₹46 per kg**

**Exam trap:** Do not take the simple average of ₹40 and ₹50 when the quantities differ. The correct average is ₹46, not ₹45.

## 3. Missing Value

When the average and some observations are given, first calculate the total, then subtract the known values.

**Formula:**

Missing value = (Average × Number of values) − Sum of known values

### Example 1: Find the Sixth Number

The average of 6 numbers is 24. Five numbers are 18, 20, 22, 25 and 27. Find the sixth number.

Total of all 6 numbers = 6 × 24 = 144

Sum of known numbers = 18 + 20 + 22 + 25 + 27 = 112

Missing number = 144 − 112 = 32

**Answer: 32**

### Example 2: Find the Tenth Student's Marks

The average marks of 10 students is 65. The average of 9 students is 63. Find the tenth student's marks.

Total marks of 10 students = 10 × 65 = 650

Total marks of 9 students = 9 × 63 = 567

Tenth student's marks = 650 − 567 = 83

**Answer: 83 marks**

**Fast method:** Whenever two averages are given for different group sizes, calculate both totals and take their difference.

## 4. Average Age

Average-age questions use the same formula as ordinary averages. The important point is that everyone's age increases by the same amount over time.

### Case A: Average Age After Some Years

The average age of 5 people is 24 years. What will their average age be after 4 years?

Each person's age increases by 4 years, so the average also increases by 4.

New average = 24 + 4 = 28 years

**Answer: 28 years**

### Case B: A New Person Joins

The average age of 4 people is 25 years. A fifth person joins, and the average becomes 27 years. Find the new person's age.

Old total = 4 × 25 = 100

New total = 5 × 27 = 135

New person's age = 135 − 100 = 35 years

**Answer: 35 years**

### Case C: A Person Leaves

The average age of 6 people is 30 years. One person leaves, and the average of the remaining 5 people becomes 28 years. Find the age of the person who left.

Old total = 6 × 30 = 180

New total = 5 × 28 = 140

Age of person who left = 180 − 140 = 40 years

**Answer: 40 years**

**Common trap:** If everyone grows older by 5 years, the average increases by 5 years, not by 5 multiplied by the number of people.

## 5. Average Marks

Average-marks problems commonly involve students, subjects, pass marks, and changes in marks.

### Case A: Find Total Marks

The average marks of 40 students is 72.

Total marks = 40 × 72 = 2,880

**Answer: 2,880 marks**

### Case B: Required Marks to Achieve a Target Average

A student has an average of 68 after 5 tests. What must they score in the sixth test to achieve an average of 70?

Existing total = 5 × 68 = 340

Required total = 6 × 70 = 420

Required sixth-test score = 420 − 340 = 80

**Answer: 80 marks**

### Case C: One Student's Marks Were Recorded Incorrectly

The average marks of 20 students was calculated as 64. Later, one student's marks were corrected from 45 to 65. Find the corrected average.

Increase in total marks = 65 − 45 = 20

Increase in average = 20 / 20 = 1

Corrected average = 64 + 1 = 65

**Answer: 65 marks**

**Shortcut:** If one value changes by d and there are n observations:

Change in average = d / n

## 6. Combined Average

A combined average is the average of two or more groups taken together.

**Formula:**

Combined average = Total of all groups / Total number of observations

For two groups:

Combined average = [(n1 × A1) + (n2 × A2)] / (n1 + n2)

Where:

- n1 and n2 = sizes of the two groups
    
- A1 and A2 = average values of the two groups
    

### Example 1: Two Classes

Class A has 30 students with an average of 60 marks. Class B has 20 students with an average of 75 marks. Find the combined average.

|Group|Students|Average|Total marks|
|---|---|---|---|
|Class A|30|60|1,800|
|Class B|20|75|1,500|
|Total|50|—|3,300|

Combined average = 3,300 / 50 = 66

**Answer: 66 marks**

### Example 2: Average Salary

The average salary of 40 employees is ₹30,000, and the average salary of 60 employees is ₹40,000. Find the overall average salary.

Total salary of first group = 40 × 30,000 = ₹12,00,000

Total salary of second group = 60 × 40,000 = ₹24,00,000

Combined total salary = ₹36,00,000

Total employees = 40 + 60 = 100

Combined average = 36,00,000 / 100 = ₹36,000

**Answer: ₹36,000**

**Exam trap:** Do not simply average the two group averages unless both groups have the same number of observations.

For equal-sized groups, the combined average is the average of their averages. For unequal groups, use group sizes as weights.

## 7. Useful Shortcuts and Common Traps

|Situation|Quick method|
|---|---|
|Average and count given|Multiply to find total|
|Missing observation|Total − Known sum|
|New member joins|New total − Old total|
|Member leaves|Old total − New total|
|Everyone ages by t years|Average increases by t years|
|One value changes by d|Average changes by d / n|
|Two groups have different sizes|Use weighted combined average|
|Consecutive/equally spaced numbers|Average = (First + Last) / 2|

### Quick Mental Example

The average of 9 numbers is 40. One number, 22, is replaced by 49. What is the new average?

Increase in total = 49 − 22 = 27

Increase in average = 27 / 9 = 3

New average = 40 + 3 = 43

**Answer: 43**
