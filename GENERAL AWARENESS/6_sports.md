### C. Sports facts to memorise

- Olympics: Summer and Winter editions; the host city, year, mascot and Indian medal winners are common revision points.
    
- Asian Games: Multisport event for Asian countries and territories.
    
- Commonwealth Games: Multisport event involving Commonwealth teams.
    
- Paralympics: International multisport event for athletes with eligible disabilities.
    
- Chess: Learn the current world champion and major Indian achievements.
    
- Cricket: Revise the winners and runners-up of the latest ICC men's and women's tournaments.
    
- Indian sports awards: Know the Major Dhyan Chand Khel Ratna and Arjuna Award.
    

Exam strategy: Make a one-page table of the latest tournament, host, winner, runner-up and Indian achievement. These details change frequently, so don't rely on old notes for current winners.

### D. UNESCO heritage

For UNESCO-related questions, revise the distinction between:

- World Heritage Sites — cultural, natural or mixed heritage.
    
- Intangible Cultural Heritage — living traditions, performing arts, rituals, craftsmanship and related knowledge.
    
- Memory of the World — documentary heritage.
    

India's UNESCO listings can change, so verify the latest additions using the official [UNESCO World Heritage List](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/) and [Intangible Cultural Heritage Lists](https://ich.unesco.org/en/lists).

### C. National events

Revise developments concerning:

- Major government policies and new programmes.
    
- Parliament and constitutional developments.
    
- Important appointments and resignations.
    
- Major infrastructure projects.
    
- National awards and honours.

### D. International events

Revise developments concerning:

- G20, BRICS, G7 and other major summits.
    
- UN decisions, resolutions and leadership changes.
    
- Wars, peace agreements and diplomatic developments.
    
- International elections and leadership changes.
    
- Global health emergencies and climate agreements.
    
- Major discoveries, scientific awards and sporting championships.
    

Current-affairs rule: For each major event, know the what, where, when and who. Do not memorise an unverified social-media headline as a fact.
