
## 1. Position from Left and Right (बाएँ और दाएँ से स्थान)

When a person is ranked from both ends of a row, use the following formula:

**Total persons = Left position + Right position − 1**

Here:

- L = Position from the left
    
- R = Position from the right
    
- N = Total number of persons
    

We subtract 1 because the same person is counted from both ends.

### Solved Example 1

Rahul is 8th from the left and 12th from the right. How many people are in the row?

**Solution:**

N = 8 + 12 − 1 = 19

**Answer: 19 people.**

### Solved Example 2

A student is 15th from the left in a row of 40 students. What is the student's position from the right?

**Formula:**

R = N − L + 1

**Solution:**

R = 40 − 15 + 1 = 26

**Answer: 26th from the right.**

### Formula Recap

|What to Find|Formula|
|---|---|
|Total persons|N = L + R − 1|
|Right position|R = N − L + 1|
|Left position|L = N − R + 1|

## 2. Position from Top and Bottom (ऊपर और नीचे से स्थान)

These questions use the same logic as left-right ranking, but the arrangement is vertical—for example, students ranked by height or people ranked in a competition.

### Solved Example 1

A student ranks 7th from the top and 18th from the bottom. Find the total number of students.

**Solution:**

N = 7 + 18 − 1 = 24

**Answer: 24 students.**

### Solved Example 2

A player is ranked 11th from the top in a list of 35 players. What is the player's rank from the bottom?

**Solution:**

Rank from bottom = 35 − 11 + 1 = 25

**Answer: 25th from the bottom.**

**Remember:** Top-bottom questions follow exactly the same formulas as left-right questions. Only the direction of ranking changes.

## 3. Finding the Total Number of Persons (कुल व्यक्तियों की संख्या)

Sometimes a question gives the number of people ahead of or behind a person rather than the rank directly.

### Important Rules

- Persons ahead + 1 = Rank from the front.
    
- Persons behind + 1 = Rank from the back.
    
- If both ranks are known, total = Front rank + Back rank − 1.
    

### Solved Example 1: People Ahead and Behind

There are 9 people ahead of Amit and 14 people behind him. How many people are in the row?

**Solution:**

Total = People ahead + Amit + People behind

N = 9 + 1 + 14 = 24

**Answer: 24 people.**

### Solved Example 2: Total from Two Ranks

Priya is 10th from the left and 16th from the right. How many people are there?

**Solution:**

N = 10 + 16 − 1 = 25

**Answer: 25 people.**

### Solved Example 3: People Between Two Persons

A is 8th from the left. B is 12th from the left. How many people stand between them?

**Solution:**

People between = Difference in ranks − 1

= 12 − 8 − 1

= 3

**Answer: 3 people.**

**Remember:** Subtract 1 from the difference between the ranks to exclude the two people at the endpoints.

## 4. Rank Interchange (स्थान बदलना)

These questions involve two people exchanging positions. You must determine the new rank, original rank, or total number of people.

### Case A: Two People Interchange Positions

When two people exchange positions, each person occupies the other's original position.

**Important:** The total number of people can be calculated using N = L + R − 1 only when the required ranks from opposite ends are known for the relevant positions.

### Solved Example 1: Finding Original Rank

Amit is 7th from the left. After exchanging positions with Rahul, Amit becomes 19th from the left. Find Rahul's original rank and the total number of people.

**Solution:**

- Amit's original rank = 7th from the left.
    
- Amit's new rank = 19th from the left.
    
- After the exchange, Amit occupies Rahul's original position.
    

Therefore, Rahul's original rank was 19th from the left.

**Answer:** Rahul was originally 19th from the left.

**Important:** The total number of people cannot be determined from the given information alone.

### Solved Example 2: Rank from Opposite Ends

A is 9th from the left. B is 15th from the right. They exchange positions, and A becomes 15th from the right. Find the total number of people.

**Solution:**

After the exchange, A occupies B's original position. Therefore, B's original position is 15th from the right, while A's original position was 9th from the left.

Total = Left position + Right position − 1

N = 9 + 15 − 1 = 23

**Answer: 23 people.**

### Case B: Rank Changes by a Fixed Number

A student moves from 18th position to 11th position from the top. By how many places did the student improve?

**Solution:**

Places improved = Old rank − New rank

= 18 − 11 = 7

**Answer: 7 places.**

If the student moves from 11th to 18th position, the student drops by 7 places.

### Solved Example 3: A Complete Interchange Question

A is 16th from the left. B is 18th from the right. After exchanging positions, A becomes 25th from the left. Find the total number of people.

**Solution:**

After the exchange, A occupies B's original position. Therefore, B's original rank from the left was 25th.

- Left position = 25
    
- Right position = 18
    

N = 25 + 18 − 1 = 42

**Answer: 42 people.**

### Rank-Interchange Shortcut

1. Identify the original position of each person.
    
2. Determine whose position is occupied after the exchange.
    
3. Establish the rank of that position from the relevant end.
    
4. Use N = L + R − 1 when ranks from opposite ends are available.
    

**Exam trap:** Do not confuse a person's original rank with the rank of the position they occupy after exchanging places.

## 5. Quick Formula Sheet

|Question Type|Formula|
|---|---|
|Total from left and right ranks|N = L + R − 1|
|Right rank from total and left rank|R = N − L + 1|
|Left rank from total and right rank|L = N − R + 1|
|Persons between two ranks from the same end|Difference − 1|
|Total persons when people ahead and behind are given|Ahead + Behind + 1|
|Places improved|Old rank − New rank|
|Places dropped|New rank − Old rank|

## 6. Final Exam Tips

1. Always subtract 1 when calculating the total from ranks at opposite ends.
    
2. Use the same formulas for top-bottom and left-right ranking.
    
3. If people ahead and behind are given, count the person in question as well.
    
4. For people standing between two ranks, subtract 1 from the difference.
    
5. In interchange questions, identify the original position occupied by the other person.
    
6. A change in rank does not reveal the total number of people unless additional information is provided.
    
7. For improvement, subtract the new rank from the old rank.
    
8. For a drop, subtract the old rank from the new rank.
